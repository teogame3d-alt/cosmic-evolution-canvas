Desktop: mouse for direction, WASD/arrows to steer, Space to brake, P to pause, R to restart, click for supernova. Mobile: use the virtual joystick for steering, Pulse for supernova, or drag/tap inside the canvas. Smaller bodies orbit and can be absorbed; only bodies larger than you deal damage.
